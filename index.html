How it signals

A trade needs a trigger and a score of six or more.

The trigger is a break of structure, a change of character, or a retest of an order block. The score counts how many of nine conditions agree. You can change the minimum score.

  • 01EMA 20 above EMA 50 and price above the slow EMA
  • 02Price on the right side of the SMA 200
  • 03MACD line and histogram agree
  • 04RSI above 50 and below overbought (mirrored for shorts)
  • 05Market structure points the same way
  • 06Buyers (or sellers) control the recent volume
  • 07Price on the right side of the Bollinger basis
  • 08A structure or order block trigger in the last five bars
  • 09Entry in discount for longs, premium for shorts
Entry
At the close of the signal candle, marked BUY or SELL.
Stop loss
Beyond the order block or last swing. If neither fits, ATR × 1.5.
Targets
TP1 at 1.5R and TP2 at 3R. The stop can move to breakeven after TP1.
Alerts
Long, short, bullish structure break and bearish structure break.

What to know before buying. TradingView does not give scripts tick data, so order flow is estimated by splitting each candle's volume into buying and selling. It is an approximation, not a footprint chart. RSI and MACD feed the score and the dashboard but are not drawn in separate panes. Volume features need an instrument that reports volume. Test it on your own market and timeframe before you trade real money.

Setup guide

On your chart in about two minutes.

You receive plain text Pine Script files. They work in TradingView on web, desktop and the phone app (editing code is easiest on a computer or tablet).

1. Add the indicator

Open a chart on TradingView, open the Pine Editor tab at the bottom, delete the sample code, paste the VenusAlgo file, then press Save and Add to chart.

2. Choose a display mode

Open the indicator settings (the gear). Display Mode switches between Full Analysis and Entry / SL / TP Only. Every layer also has its own switch.

3. Recommended starting settings

Start on the 15 minute or 1 hour chart of a liquid pair such as BTCUSD. Keep the minimum score at 6 of 9. Raise it to 7 for fewer, stronger signals. Test on the replay tool before risking money.

4. Set alerts

Right-click the chart, choose Add alert, pick VenusAlgo as the condition and choose Long, Short or a structure break. Alerts fire when the candle closes.

5. Using the strategy (Pro bundle)

Add the strategy the same way, then open the Strategy Tester tab to see the trade list and equity curve. Backtests use past data and do not predict future results.

Learn SMC

The ideas behind the signals.

Short plain-language notes on the concepts VenusAlgo draws, so you know what you are looking at.

What is an order block?

An order block is the last opposite candle before a strong move that breaks structure. Traders watch it as an area where large orders may have been placed, so price sometimes reacts when it returns.

VenusAlgo draws these zones and removes them once price closes through them.

BOS vs CHoCH

A break of structure (BOS) is price closing beyond the last swing high or low in the direction of the trend, so the trend continues. A change of character (CHoCH) is the first break against the trend and is an early sign it may be turning.

What is a liquidity sweep?

Many stop orders sit just above old highs and below old lows. A sweep is when price briefly pokes through that level and then closes back inside. It can mean those orders were taken and price may reverse.

Fair value gaps and premium / discount

A fair value gap is a three-candle pattern where the first and third candles do not overlap, leaving an imbalance price may revisit. Premium and discount split the current range at its midpoint: buying in the lower half (discount) and selling in the upper half (premium) gives a better price.

Why a score instead of one signal?

No single condition is reliable alone. VenusAlgo counts how many of nine conditions agree (trend, momentum, structure, volume, location) and only draws a trade when enough of them do.

After you send the payment and email your proof, we confirm it on the blockchain and email the Pine Script files to the address you wrote from. Delivery is usually within 24 hours.

Does it repaint?

Signals are built to confirm on the close of a candle, and the higher-timeframe data in the strategy is offset so it does not peek at unfinished bars. Always check any tool yourself on the replay feature.

Does it guarantee profit?

No. It is an educational charting tool. Trading is risky and you can lose money. A losing trade is normal for every system, which is why each signal comes with a stop loss.

Is the order flow real?

TradingView does not give scripts tick data, so buyer and seller volume is estimated from each candle. It is useful for context but is not true order book data.

Which timeframe and markets?

The 15 minute and 1 hour charts are good places to start on liquid markets such as BTC, ETH and major forex pairs. Lower timeframes give more signals and more noise.
